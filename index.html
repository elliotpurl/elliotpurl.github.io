About Elliot

Finding a therapist can be an intimidating process. It’s complicated and vulnerable having to lay yourself out to a stranger, never mind finding the right fit for what you need. My practice is grounded in transparency and honesty. I believe you’re the expert in your own experience, which means you guide the entire treatment process.

My priority is building a space where you can be authentically yourself- no apologies, no pressure to be put together, no “right answer.” We’re all human, and deserve to be listened to. In that way, documentation matters. I want to make sure that you’re in control of what you want documented, or which diagnoses are on your record, rather than having to wonder.

Together, we can explore your concerns and goals and find a path forward!
