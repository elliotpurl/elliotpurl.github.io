COVID-19 precautions

As someone who lives in a household of disabled individuals, I understand what it means to be personally responsible for reducing the risk of illness transmission. I also have personal experience with chronic illness and chronic pain.

As an independent contractor, in-person services with Mindful Therapy Group are not inherently COVID-safe. However, I have an air purifier (WINIX C710) and use dedicated buffer time before clients arrive to ensure air quality in the office space I use during and between sessions with clients. I wear N95s around everyone except my household members, and test for COVID weekly. I also have telehealth availability.

I ask that all my clients reach out to reschedule or switch to telehealth, should any symptoms of illness arise before an appointment.
